About me

Beauty is not only injections and surface procedures — it is also inner health.

I'm Nadezda Pravorova, a physician of the 1st category. Dermatocosmetologist, trichologist and nutritionist with 13 years of experience in clinical medicine plus 4 years of working with clients in private and online practice. I graduated from Grodno State Medical University with a degree in General Medicine. I completed specialisations in dermatovenereology, trichology and cosmetology. I finished the University of Educational Medicine course in Integrative and Clinical Nutrition.

I work within the concept of an "individual, comprehensive approach" — there are no two identical bodies, which means there are no identical protocols. The health and beauty of our skin and hair is a mirror of our overall health.

Approach

My philosophy of health and beauty

I'm convinced that the body is always speaking to us — through skin breakouts, hair loss, digestion, hormonal disruptions, low energy, anxiety or chronic inflammation. And behind every symptom there is a cause that is important to see.

My approach is an attentive, comprehensive review of the whole person: not just lab tests, but also lifestyle, nutrition, stress levels, sleep, emotional state and the body's inner resources.

I combine knowledge of medicine and nutrition with hands-on experience guiding people back to health. No extremes, no rigid bans, no one-size-fits-all schemes. With respect for the body, its rhythm and each person's individuality.

My work is to help you not just "treat symptoms", but to truly reclaim your health, energy and quality of life.
